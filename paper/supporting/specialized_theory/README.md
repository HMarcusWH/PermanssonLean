# Specialized supporting theory

This directory contains the five Hermansson working papers cited in **Appendix E — Specialized Finite-Certificate and Rigidity Extensions** of Permansson Regimes v0.1.8.

These papers provide theorem architectures for restricted subclasses with additional linear, bilinear, nested, singular, or quadratic structure. They are **not universal GR/PR results**. Transfer into a Permansson subclass requires the subclass to satisfy the relevant hypotheses and a direct theorem to establish the transfer.

## Referenced papers

- **Hermansson (2026b)** — [*Scalar-Defect Reduction for Finite Weil–CCM Dictionaries*](Scalar_Defect_Reduction_Finite_Weil_CCM_Dictionaries_2026-08-30_v0_1_PROOFREAD.pdf), 30 August 2026. Used by the **scalar-defect track**: under additional linear/quadratic rigidity, a symmetric representation discrepancy may collapse to a low-rank or scalar common-mode defect.
- **Hermansson (2026c)** — [*Persistent Finite Detection of Off-Critical Zeros in the Weil–CCM Hierarchy*](Persistent_Finite_Detection_Off_Critical_Zeros_Weil_CCM_2026-09-03_v0_2.pdf), 3 September 2026. Used by the **persistent finite-detection track**: strict full-object failure can transfer to legal finite approximants only under the required continuity and nested-embedding conditions.
- **Hermansson (2026d)** — [*First-Bad Cubic Rigidity and Source-Explicit Cross-Parity Secular Transfer in the Weil–CCM Hierarchy*](First_Bad_Cubic_Rigidity_Weil_CCM_2026-09-08_v0_2.pdf), 8 September 2026. Used by the **first-bad reduction track**: the obstruction dimension follows the successor quotient dimension; scalar reduction is licensed only in the one-dimensional case.
- **Hermansson (2026e)** — [*Zero-Shift Source Rigidity and Kernel Annihilation in the Finite Weil–CCM Hierarchy*](Zero_Shift_Source_Rigidity_Weil_CCM_2026-09-10_v0_3.pdf), 10 September 2026. Used by the **singular-compatibility track**: singular response or predecessor operators require kernel/range compatibility rather than an unjustified inverse or pseudoinverse.
- **Hermansson (2026f)** — [*Explicit Gram Witnesses for Off-Critical Pair Blocks in Finite Weil Quadratic Forms*](Explicit_Gram_Witnesses_Off_Critical_Pair_Blocks_2026-08-25_v1_2_PROOFREAD.pdf), 25 August 2026. Used by the **constructive quadratic-witness track**: in the signed rank-one quadratic subclass, an explicit Gram witness supplies a separating direction and perturbation margin.

## Scope boundary

Appendix E explicitly keeps these results outside the universal GR/PR theorem set. Analogy alone does not promote a specialized Weil–CCM result into Permansson Regime Theory.

These PDFs are repository-hosted copies of cited supporting papers and were added after the frozen v0.1.8 release-package lineage; they do not alter the immutable release hashes or Lean snapshot.
