# All-initial compact Feller empirical limits

The six modules listed by `audit_compact_feller.py` prove empirical cluster
invariance for the actual canonical Markov law on a nonempty compact metric
Borel space, for every initial probability law.

`BoundedSquareStrongLaw` proves a square-subsequence estimate and bounded-gap
interpolation. `BoundedInnovations` and `CanonicalBoundedInnovations` construct
actual conditional-mean-zero innovations of the canonical law, establish their
second-moment bounds and derive the required strong law. No stationarity,
finite-state or recurrence premise supplies that strong law.

`CompactFellerKernel` proves weak continuity of the kernel action, closedness
of the invariant-law set and extension from dense continuous tests.
`CompactFellerEmpirical` intersects a countable supnorm-dense family in `C(Y)`
before quantifying over cluster laws. Compactness supplies actual cluster
points, Feller continuity makes them invariant, and uniqueness yields full
weak convergence. An actual original typed occupation-clause adapter retains
the original almost-sure-weak mode and independent Exact-GR obligations.

`CompactFellerDistance` gives literal distance-to-invariant-family convergence
for a fixed compatible weak metric. It proves the equivalence with cluster
containment for nonempty closed predeclared families, and singleton recovery.
Continuous descriptors push those conclusions forward. The family is fixed
before observing the trajectory.

The positive witness uses a continuum compact interval with a reset kernel.
An identity kernel retains two distinct invariant Dirac laws. All six sources,
the witness and the 88 exported-declaration audit passed; the discovered
wrapper runs both checks. Only the three standard Lean axioms are allowed.

Stationary random-law and absolutely-continuous extensions are separate
applications. A noncompact marginal bound is not substituted for almost-sure
tightness, and no countable supnorm-dense family in all of noncompact `C_b(Y)`
is asserted.
