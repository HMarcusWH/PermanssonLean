finite products of measurable component evaluations, and the pinned Giry
measure theorem extends that to every measurable set. All product rows are
proved normalized.

The construction reuses the existing finite-index mechanism/generator data,
assembles the actual original strategic generator and retains the literal
physical world. The actual alpha/P/U integral formula is proved. When state and
action coordinates happen to be finite, the generator and full typed model
equal the earlier implementation, preserving its binary full-law bridges.
The binary product measure reduction itself works for arbitrary measurable
coordinate spaces under the actual measurable coordinate equivalence.

`MeasurableProductProtocols` defines selected-player closed-loop and open-loop
replacements. Both use the same replacement at the treated coordinate; the

physical world remain the specified objects. The original complete comparison
set and property enter the original margin comparison theorem directly.

The regression uses three players with real-valued private states and actions.
It checks different opponent action cylinders, identical treated action and
update cylinders, unchanged common/world kernels and distinct actual joint
action laws. The empty-index product is also checked: it has one empty tuple,
and a treated player cannot be selected from an empty index set.
