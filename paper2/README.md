# Permansson Regimes - second edition

**Marcus Hermansson. _Permansson Regimes: Structure, Intervention, and Computation Beyond Equilibrium_. Reviewed manuscript draft v2.0.1, 20 November 2026.**

The second edition brings the original generated-regime and constitution framework together with the repository's later probability, occupation, intervention, control, game, causal, algebraic and executable developments. It is **218 pages**, with **31 main sections and 16 integral appendices**. Its remaining mathematical and evidence obligations are stated in section 31 and Appendix P.
