# Current glossary: manuscript v2.0.1

[Current documentation](README.md) · [Mathematical crosswalk](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md) · [Compatibility and corrections](COMPATIBILITY_AND_CORRECTIONS.md)

This glossary is a reading aid for the reviewed second edition. The exact paper statements and source declarations govern their own hypotheses. The literal label in the last column can be looked up in the [reviewed label index](../../paper2/v2.0.1/indices/manuscript_label_index.json).

| Term | Reading meaning | Paper route and literal label |
|---|---|---|
| **Strategic-world model** | The typed process with action-selection kernel α, physical world kernel P and strategic update kernel U, composed in that chronological order. The strategic generator is the declared pair (α, U). | [§3](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c03); `sec:C03` |
| **Frozen regime specification** | The declared region, basin, descriptor, reference data and convergence requirement used to classify a process. The mathematical input does not establish when an analyst selected it. | [§4](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c04); `eq:C04-spec` |
| **Exact generated regime (GR)** | The original process-relative classification combining its nontriviality assumptions, exact region invariance and the stated descriptor-occupation limit from the declared basin. | [§4](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c04); `def:C04-GR` |
| **Finite persistence and quasi-regime** | A finite-horizon retention assertion with its declared failure tolerance and start domain. Its finite scope does not supply exact invariance or an infinite-horizon conclusion. | [§4](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c04); `def:C04-quasi` |
| **Quasi-stationary distribution (QSD)** | A law whose killed-process evolution is proportional to itself. Its survival statement is law-specific; uniform finite persistence over an entire region needs its own hypotheses. | [§4](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c04); `def:C04-QSD` |
| **Permansson regime (PR)** | A regime equipped with a legal strategic intervention and a change in the declared property over the required comparison domain. Its meaning is relative to the intervention family and property. | [§5](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c05); `def:C05-PR` |
| **Grounded property** | A property related through measurable grounding maps under the stated comparison law. Nuisance padding cannot manufacture the grounded change excluded by the theorem. | [§5](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c05); `eq:C05-groundingmaps` |
| **Constitutive margin** | The infimum of the declared property-distance effect across the specified comparison starts. The frozen full comparison domain and absolute distance matter. | [§5](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c05); `eq:C05-margin` |
| **Random occupation law and barycenter** | A trajectory can choose a random recurrent class and hence a random limit law. The distribution of that limit and its average measure are different objects, especially for nonlinear properties. | [§7](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c07), [§21](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c21); `thm:C07-random-limit` |
| **Admitted grammar** | The family of legal patch blocks whose composition is defined on the actual typed inputs and holds the specified primitives fixed. Set-theoretic subsets need not all be admitted. | [§11](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c11); `def:C11:grammar` |
| **Minimal positive block** | An admitted positive block with no strictly smaller admitted positive block. Several incomparable minimal positive blocks can coexist. | [§11](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c11); `def:C11:minimal` |
| **Least positive block** | An admitted block that is itself positive and is contained in every positive admitted block. The containment condition alone omits an essential requirement. | [§11](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c11); `def:C11:minimal` |
| **Finite constitutive certificate** | A scoped certificate combining declared finite persistence, the admissible intervention/comparison structure and a positive finite-property margin, with the transport hypotheses kept explicit. | [§12](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c12); `eq:C12:certificate` |
| **Finite-prefix versus whole-path error** | A bound on a fixed observation prefix and a bound on the complete path sigma-field have different premises. Infinite common-mass retention requires its own defect conditions. | [§13](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c13); `sec:C13` |
| **Metric and pseudometric** | A pseudometric may assign zero distance to distinct objects. Conclusions using separation, such as uniqueness, require the actual metric profile stated in the theorem. | [§14](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c14), [§15](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c15); `sec:C14` |
| **Descriptor nonconstancy on a region** | The descriptor takes at least two values on the declared region. This is a predicate about the descriptor and region. | [§4](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c04), [§23](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c23); `sec:C04` |
| **Reachable descriptor-law nondegeneracy** | The basin-reachable descriptor laws include at least two distinct probability measures, possibly at different times from the same start. This is a different predicate from descriptor nonconstancy on a region. | [§4](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c04), [§23](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c23); `def:C04-confirmatory` |
| **Four-process factorization** | The required property-factorization assertions must hold for all four actual kernels in the original/perturbed baseline/intervention comparison. Supplying only three does not establish the fourth. | [§15](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c15); `thm:C15:margin` |
| **Identified-set constitution** | A claim evaluated over a declared set of models compatible with the evidence and assumptions. A conclusion about one fitted or constructed model need not hold throughout that set. | [§28](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c28); `def:identified-constitution` |
| **Simultaneous coverage** | A stated probability bound for the joint event that the relevant true object lies in the random uncertainty set. Nonemptiness, measurability and the actual coverage premise must be supplied. | [§28](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c28); `thm:outer-certificate` |
| **Deterministic completion bounds** | Bounds over allowed completions of missing coordinates or observations. They require their declared normalization/range conditions and do not themselves provide a sampling-coverage probability. | [§28](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c28); `prop:completion-bounds` |
| **Numerical receipt** | A strict record containing the declared input identity, witness, comparison conditions and checkable numerical relations. Its validity is assessed by the specified checker and scope. | [§29](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c29); `def:receipt-contract` |
| **Reflected instance** | A bounded concrete object reified into Lean and linked to the theorem appropriate to that exact object. A successfully checked instance retains its input and reflection limits. | [§29](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c29); `prop:reflection-chain` |
| **Evidence coordinates** | Separate records for source identity, literal statement/assumptions, manuscript derivation, correspondence, compiler execution, numerical execution, adequacy, independent review and empirical identification. Support in one field does not automatically fill another. | [§30](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c30); `def:evidence-coordinates` |
| **Source route and semantic equivalence** | A source route locates a file, declaration or proof group. Equivalence review compares actual statements and hypotheses; a successful path or label lookup is only structural evidence. | [§30](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md#c30); `def:coverage-disposition` |

## Certificate coordinates and legacy vocabulary

Section 28.1 uses nine **distinct reporting coordinates** for the scientific certificate. Distinct coordinate fields are not an assertion that their contents, errors or estimates are statistically independent. The [Application Standard](../../application/APPLICATION_STANDARD.md) remains the authority for the older draft reporting contract and its wire vocabulary; the [application compatibility page](APPLICATION_COMPATIBILITY.md) describes the relation to current application outputs.

The preserved [earlier glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) describes its historical paper/contract context. Its unqualified section references should be read in that edition. Use the current glossary and [version lineage](VERSION_LINEAGE.md) for this manuscript.
