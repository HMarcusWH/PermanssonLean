## Maintenance rule

A new guide or manuscript edition needs a new source-qualified mapping review. Retain all original IDs and edition hashes, validate actual paper labels and source paths, and state whether a relationship is direct statement correspondence or editorial topic navigation. The public [paper label index](../../paper2/v2.0.1/indices/manuscript_label_index.json) and [mathematical crosswalk](MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md) supply the destinations used here. The [documentation review](DOCUMENTATION_REVIEW.md) records the implemented check results.
