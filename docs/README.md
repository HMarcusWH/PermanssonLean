## Choose a reading path

**Reader:** start with the [current manuscript](../paper2/README.md), [reading routes](paper2/GETTING_STARTED.md) and [current glossary](paper2/GLOSSARY.md). Reading the PDF needs no installation. Use the [original paper guide](../paper/README.md) for the frozen v0.1.8 edition.

**Application-report author:** identify the applicable contract through [application compatibility](paper2/APPLICATION_COMPATIBILITY.md). For the historical reporting standard, follow the preserved [setup and first example](GETTING_STARTED.md), [seven fixtures](../application/examples/README.md) and [canonical standard](../application/APPLICATION_STANDARD.md). Contract validation and empirical identification remain separate.

**Proof-replay user:** consult the native [C10 six-state](../apps/permansson/closure-successor/README.md) or [C11 finite stationary-family](../apps/permansson/closure-successor-c11/README.md) routes, or the [successor application's guide](../apps/permansson/application-successor/README.md). Their supported objects, compiler evidence and output fields retain their own scope.

**Proof reviewer or research mathematician:** use the [current mathematical crosswalk](paper2/MATHEMATICAL_CROSSWALK.md), [residual status](paper2/RESIDUAL_STATUS.md) and [research build guide](RESEARCH_BUILD.md). The [original v0.1.8 ledger](FORMALIZATION_MAP.md#source-to-lean-theorem-and-definition-ledger), [Lean provenance](../verification/lean/README.md), [dated integration index](V020_IMPLEMENTATION_INDEX.md) and [closure scope records](../research/paper_closures/README.md) keep their historical authority; the current maps explain later source routes and the remaining reconciliation requirements.

**Reproducer or contributor:** follow [current reproduction](paper2/VERIFICATION.md) and [contribution routes](paper2/GETTING_STARTED.md#contribute-to-the-current-documentation), alongside the preserved [contribution requirements](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Choose the operation corresponding to the claim being checked and record its actual source and result.
